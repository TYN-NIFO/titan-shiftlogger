## Use case: AI-enabled shift logger
- Titan runs 2 shifts. The department chosen for the pilot has about 80 operators across the 2 shifts.
- Each operator fills in a job card at the end of the shift.
- The shift supervisor copies the job cards into a logbook.
- A data-entry person types the logbook into an Excel sheet.
- The Excel sheet drives data visualisations and reports for management.

**Objective:** an AI tool with a chatbot front end that turns job cards into structured reports and gives management simple, consolidated answers.

**Example prompts**
1. Identify the downtime of the CNC machine over the last 4 months.
2. Which operators have the lowest productivity?
3. Which operator has the highest absenteeism?
4. Give the initial troubleshooting steps for a given machine before calling the technician.

**Key constraint:** the data is core Titan proprietary information and must stay secure. Public LLMs are questionable, so the solution needs an **on-premise, asset-light LLM**.
